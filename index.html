JOSHWAY & the Courage to Rise

JOSHWAY and the Courage to Rise follows one boy who discovers that true strength isn’t about being tough. It’s about facing big feelings and having the courage to ask for help. Brave choices, big heart, and a message every child needs to hear: you can be the hero of your own story.

Buy a book, change a life. Every $15.99 paperback supports JOSHWAY®, the Easton, PA nonprofit helping young people build life skills and confidently find a pathway forward.
