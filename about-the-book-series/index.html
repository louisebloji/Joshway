WHY THE BOOKS EXIST

Every child deserves language for what they carry.

The JOSHWAY Book Project turns meaningful life skills into stories families and educators can share together—one page, question, and brave conversation at a time.

01

Name big feelings

Give children approachable language for emotions that can otherwise feel too large or confusing to explain.

02

Make asking easier

Show that reaching out is not weakness—it is a thoughtful, courageous step toward support and connection.

03

Open a pathway

Help young readers see that their next brave choice can become the beginning of a new way forward.
