FULL BIOGRAPHY

Melody Bradford is an Emmy Award-winning producer, sales executive, and host of Trailblazers on WDIY, an NPR-affiliated station. The podcast amplifies stories of courage, leadership, and hope.

Her professional journey spans broadcasting, sales, and publishing, including work with Rodale Publishing in support of New York Times bestselling franchises such as The South Beach Diet, The Abs Diet, and the Men’s Health and Women’s Health brands.

Guided by a deep belief in the power of storytelling to heal and empower, Melody brings a heart-centered perspective to this work—advocating for children navigating change, adversity, and transition while helping illuminate paths toward resilience and possibility.
